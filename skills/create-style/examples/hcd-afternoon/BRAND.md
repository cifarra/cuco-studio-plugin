# Hialeah City Dental — "Afternoon" (v2)

## Brief (context)

**What it is.** A family and cosmetic dental clinic at 1544 W 37th St, Hialeah, FL.
They offer exams, emergencies, smile design, implants and orthodontics, plus their
own tech: digital impressions, an in-house lab and 3D imaging.

**Audience & voice.** A bilingual (EN/ES), largely Cuban-American neighbourhood.
Reviews call it "the best dental clinic in Hialeah". The voice is warm, calm,
reassuring, premium but approachable. Hero line: "Transforming smiles with
innovation, expertise, and care. *We're here for you.*" (the italic is in peach).

**Type.** Newsreader (Production Type; real @font-face, installed): text serif,
sentence case, tight tracking, with the italic as the brand gesture. Manrope for UI.

**Image language.** Warm, soft studio portraits on peach and tan backdrops (smiles,
hands on cheeks, feel-good). A slate light falls on the doctor. The tech close-ups
are a scan on a tablet, milled crowns and a panoramic X-ray.

**Left on the site:** eyebrow rules, rounded cards, the VISIT US / OPEN / CALL info
strip, pills, and fade-up reveals. v1 of this style transcribed exactly these.

## World (design)

**Material + process.** A cream stucco wall in the clinic. Afternoon sun comes
through a jalousie window, and everything is written in light and shade. This is
Hialeah's light: warm, slow, familiar. It sounds like "we're here for you" rather
than "precision dentistry".

**Empty frame.** A slanted patch of warm sun on cream plaster, louver stripes, the
cool navy-tinted shade of the room, sometimes a palm frond moving in the light.

**Signature: the light MOVES.**
- A shot opens when the sun swings onto the wall and closes when it moves on.
- Each phrase is an object approaching the wall: its shadow comes from far, blurred,
  larger and offset, and lands sharp. That is physical focus, from shadow geometry.
- Across a video the day moves from midday to golden hour: the light warms and drifts.

**Type in the world.** Newsreader as cast shadow on the sunlit wall (shadow-only 3D
layer). The landing line is written IN light through a stencil, in italic. Long copy
is painted on the wall and inks word by word as it's spoken, with the light passing
over it.

**Palette use.**
- Cream is the plaster.
- Navy is the shade: the ambient light is navy-tinted, so shadows are brand navy.
- Peach is the sunlight, its colour derived from palette 3, warming toward gold with
  the day.

**Media becomes material.** The photo is a transparency in the sun's path (Light
Transmission 100). It is projected onto the wall in sunlight, with the jalousie
stripes across it, and the blinds open and close.

**Trinity (pass 2, not wired).**
- FORM: shadow depth/softness and how literal the projection is.
- FLOW: sun drift and frond sway.
- PRESSURE: slat density and how much shade covers the frame.
