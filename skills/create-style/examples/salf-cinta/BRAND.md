# Se Acabó La Fiesta (SALF) — "Cinta" (v2)

v1 (SALF 29N) was rejected for two reasons. It converged on HCD Smile: the same
serif, the line-by-line rise, the same layouts and shot skeleton. It also baked in
content: a section counter, the "29N" date and the candidate's portrait.
v2 starts from the brand's **posters, reels and Telegram**, not its website.

## Brief (context)

**Register.** Their social output is loud, fast and confrontational:
- reel captions in heavy italic caps, with the keyword in tan
- posters that set words on **tan strips** across the frame ("ALVISE EN /
  [BARCELONA]", "[JUEVES 19 DE NOVIEMBRE]", "[PARTIDO PEQUEÑO]"), slightly tilted,
  sometimes bleeding off the edges
- event posters with a small **photo print in a white border**, tilted, stuck on the
  layout
- Telegram posts of document screenshots, underlined in marker as evidence

The editing is hard cuts, talking heads and punch-ins. The tempo is a rally's, not a
clinic's.

**Palette.** Flat, exact:

| Slot | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Night | `#0C1714` | the board (main field); text on tan/paper strips |
| 2 Paper | `#FDFAF3` | strips; print borders; text on night strips; the long-copy field |
| 3 Tan | `#D7BD96` | strips; tape; the shout field |

**Type.** Montserrat ExtraBold Italic, all caps, tight. This is the poster face; the
site's Newsreader is not used.

**Mark.** The squirrel plus "SE ACABÓ LA FIESTA", carried on a strip of tape.

**No content.** No numbers, dates, names or people. The only footage is the
squirrel mark.

## System (design)

**One device: tape (la cinta).** Words are printed on strips of tape, and photos are
taped up as prints.
- **Pull.** Each line is a strip sized to its words, with torn ends, pulled in from
  the side like tape off a roll. It is fast and decelerating, alternating sides line
  by line. It lands with a slap: a damped rotation settle and a 4% squash.
- **Rip.** When a phrase ends, its strips are ripped off, accelerating out the far
  side.
- **Print.** Story media land as prints in a paper border, dropped and tilted, with
  a damped settle. Two pieces of tape slap onto the corners. A full-bleed photo
  punches in instead.
- **Cuts.** Hard cuts, nothing leaves at the section end, so there is no empty field
  between shots.
- **Layout.** Stacks of tilted strips, centred, or left on long copy. Each line has
  its own angle from a pattern varied by the section index (design, not content).

**Trinity.**
- FORM: strip tilt (level type ↔ a steep collage).
- FLOW: tempo of pulls and rips, plus a slow drift of the board.
- PRESSURE: jitter of angle and offset, and strips overlapping.
